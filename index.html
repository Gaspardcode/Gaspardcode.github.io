About

Final-year engineering student in systems and network security. I like hard, low-level problems and systems that have to keep working, and I learn each layer by building it.

Who I am

I am in my final year of engineering at EPITA, majoring in systems and network security. When I want to understand a layer of production, I write my own version of it, run it, and watch how it breaks.

That habit gave me a compiler, a TCP/IP stack, a hypervisor and a homelab I run remotely every day. I am not tied to one job title: reliability, security, infrastructure and systems software all draw on the same skills, and I want to keep growing across them.

    5 · Observatory

    Product · in the making

    Observatory

    A search engine over every public TLS certificate, built from Certificate Transparency logs. It tells you what is about to expire or is too weak before it causes an outage.

    Expiry still causes outages

    Large services have gone down because one certificate quietly expired. Alerts on expiry are cheap; outages are not.

    Everything is already public

    Browsers require public certificates to be logged in CT. That log is a full, verifiable inventory of who has what.

    Passive by design

    Nothing is scanned. Every row comes from public logs, so the service can run in the open without probing anyone.

    SubjectIssuerKeyDays leftState

    Sample data on example domains. Next: expiry alerts for domains you prove you own, then monitoring for private PKIs.
